# Armory Vault — Subtle Black Accents

This round carries forward the v2 dark and light steel materials, texture, sheen, and layout. Small black accents refine the Sign In button, archive badge, thin search bezel, and fine hairlines. The main navigation, footer, and category bands retain their v2 steel treatment.

Desktop layouts are 1600 × 1080 and mobile layouts are 430 × 1180, with PNGs at 2×. Each 2240 × 1440 paired board places mobile immediately to the right of desktop and exports at 1.5×. The combined comparison is 2240 × 2880 at 1×.

The approved crest remains exact native vector artwork, and layout text uses outlined glyphs from the official bundled fonts. The three image assets are reused unchanged from v2; their original generation prompts are recorded in [image-prompts.md](image-prompts.md).
