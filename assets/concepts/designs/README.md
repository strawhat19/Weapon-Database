# Weapon Database Landing Designs

Design rounds for the Weapon Database landing page live here, using the official identity from `assets/logos/v1/`.

- [v1 — Armory Vault, Dossier Index, Future Systems](v1/README.md): three desktop and mobile concepts with headers, heroes, and footers.
- [v2 — Armory Vault, Charcoal Steel and Silver Steel](v2/README.md): refined dark and light designs with mobile beside desktop.
- [v3 — Armory Vault, Blackened Steel and Silver And Black](v3/README.md): deeper blacks and stronger black panels with the same desktop/mobile layouts.
- [v4 — Armory Vault, Original Steel With Subtle Black Accents](v4/README.md): v2 materials and surfaces retained, with small black controls, badges, and hairlines.
- [v5 — Armory Vault, Subtle Steel Oval Surround](v5/README.md): v3 preserved with a recessed steel oval and small black/green details around the rifle in both themes and screen sizes.
- [v6 — Armory Vault, Slightly Softer Blacks And Greens](v6/README.md): v3 designs with a tiny reduction in black/green intensity and the unchanged v5 steel oval, in dark/light desktop and mobile layouts.
- [v7 — Armory Vault, Original Steel And V5 Oval](v7/README.md): v2/v4 steel designs with slightly softer black/green accents and the unchanged v5 oval, with mobile beside desktop in both themes.
- [v8 — Armory Vault, Clean V2 Steel And V5 Oval](v8/README.md): v2 steel controls, outlined badges and fine gray rules restored, retaining muted greens and the v5 oval across both themes and screen sizes.
- [v9 — Armory Vault, Refined Archive Details](v9/README.md): v8 preserved with sparse specimen labels, fine annotations, entry indicators and small interface details in both themes and screen sizes.

Keep future rounds in the next unused `vN/` folder and preserve earlier designs.
