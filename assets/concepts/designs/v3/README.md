# Armory Vault — More Black

This round keeps the v2 layout and increases the black surfaces. The dark theme uses deeper black steel and black panels. The light theme keeps its silver-steel hero, with a black header, footer, search, Loadout Lab panel, and category band, plus white controls.

Desktop layouts are 1600 × 1080 and mobile layouts are 430 × 1180, with PNGs at 2×. The 2240 × 1440 paired boards place mobile immediately to the right of desktop and export at 1.5×. The combined comparison is 2240 × 2880 at 1×.

The approved crest, outlined official fonts, and image assets carry forward from v2. This round reuses the generated steel textures and isolated rifle; [image-prompts.md](image-prompts.md) records their origin.
