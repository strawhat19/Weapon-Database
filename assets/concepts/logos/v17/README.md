# Weapon Database — Armory Crest v17

This refinement combines v16's native mirrored geometry with the stronger black separations and weapon details selected from v15. It focuses only on the crest, using the current palette and transparent surrounding canvas.

The sword tip has a deliberate bold black V. Mirrored receiver cutouts, front collars, continuous toothed underbarrel rails, heavier stock pads and extended stock inlays give the rifles stronger definition. Three smooth gray magazine rails now run from the upper receiver edge through to the lower band; they are clipped to the magazine body. Small leading highlights retain the original silver detail.

Scope inlays are thicker, and the radial ticks now join the circular bands with clean black registration cuts. The jet has thicker black boundaries and wing slots. Its lower tail fins and center exhaust are white with black linework; the previous gray fills there are removed. Subtle mirrored gray facets sit on the rear edges of the main swept wings instead.

Paired rifles, shield details and scope geometry remain exact reflections across x = 394. The sword, guard, grip and pommel remain centered. Jet wings reflect across the jet's own x = 614 axis. The distinct bow and jet motifs retain the approved composition. The SVG uses editable named groups and native shapes, with no embedded bitmaps or font dependencies.
