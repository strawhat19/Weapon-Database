# Weapon Database — Logo Concepts v3

This round adds sword-matching silver to Armory Crest and develops Modular WD with richer detail both inside and outside its badge. Armory Crest continues as the favored primary identity.

| Direction | Editable files | Refinement |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 01 — Armory Crest | `01-armory-crest-mark.svg`, `01-armory-crest-lockup.svg` | Silver reticle spokes and ticks, silver details in the crossed rifles, a silver arrowhead, and an entirely silver fighter-jet silhouette. The sword and peaked green shield retain their hierarchy. |
| 02 — Modular WD | `02-modular-wd-mark.svg`, `02-modular-wd-lockup.svg` | Additional scope, vault-panel, bolt, and finish details around the initials inside the badge, with a fuller layered weapon-and-armor frame outside. Green WD letters and pale blade inlays remain prominent. |

| Visual refinement | Source and method |
| --- | --- |
| `03-armory-crest-silver.png` | Built-in image generation edit of the left Armory Crest panel in `../v2/03-armory-modular-refinement.png`; exact instructions in `armory-generation-prompt.txt`. |
| `04-modular-wd-expanded.png` | Built-in image generation edit of the right Modular WD panel in the same v2 image; exact instructions in `modular-generation-prompt.txt`. |

The raster images preserve the favored generated designs. The SVGs are independent editable interpretations of the requested refinements, not exact vector traces of the raster images.

| Color | Hex | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Vault Black | `#090E0B` | Emblem bodies, dark gaps, and presentation backgrounds |
| Field Olive | `#617340` | Weapons, borders, and secondary panels |
| Tactical Green | `#8AAD45` | Primary monogram and military accents |
| Structural Green | `#3E5949` | Recessed armor, vent panels, and supporting details |
| Archive Light / Silver White | `#E6EDDF` | Blades, jet, reticle spokes, arrowhead, weapon hardware, and wordmark |

The editable SVGs have transparent surrounding canvases, accessible titles and descriptions, and unique v3 IDs. Mark viewBoxes are `256 × 256`; lockup viewBoxes are `920 × 280`. Pale wordmarks are designed for a dark background, with live editable text using Arial Black, Impact, and sans-serif fallbacks.

These are detailed concept emblems intended for larger display. A simplified production mark can be developed after a direction is adopted.

All assets are new in `assets/concepts/logos/v3/`; earlier rounds are preserved. Verification, rendering checks, testing, builds, and application adoption are left to the project owner, as requested.
