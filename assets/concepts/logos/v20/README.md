The front forearm windows have chamfered outlines, a wider black collar separation, short gray collar clips and longer silver inserts. Their thin white rims follow the insert geometry. The rear windows have clipped corners and a clear green divider.

The receiver shoulders have a lower contour and a short black relief that defines the forearm connection. The metallic receiver detail sits beneath its black aperture with a distinct lower recess, following the v15 arrangement.

The trigger pockets have clean stepped contours, lower straight white bars and black clearance around the red selectors. Separate small grip recesses, broader shortened grip heels and angled black relief at the magazine mouths restore the more articulated v15 gun details. All paired rifle shapes and hardware share reflected native geometry.

The continuous 10px directional crossing channel, 52px central tang, sword-to-scope clearance, sword handle bands, stock details and other crest elements continue from v19. The directional channel preserves the chosen front/back overlap while paired gun details remain mirrored.

The SVG contains native named paths, shapes, groups and mirrored references. It has no embedded raster images or font dependencies. Its PNG is rendered directly from the same master.
