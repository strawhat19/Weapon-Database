# Weapon Database — Armory Crest v22

This round makes the three requested corrections to v21: full-width magazine separators, removal of the marked diagonal bar, and plain gray joining rails with separate black details beside them. The colors, transparent canvas and native vector construction are preserved.

The magazine separator lines extend across the complete outer collar width. Their geometry is clipped to the trigger frame, curved neck and magazine body within the collar region, so both ends finish flush with the actual silhouette. The straight angle and 4.5px thickness from v21 are retained.

The separate diagonal black crossing bar is removed entirely. The existing sword-tip V and native receiver and trigger openings remain at the joining area.

The two gray joining bars are restored to their original plain 3 × 29px shapes. Their surrounding black rectangles are removed. Two small black ports sit beside each bar, with green space separating the ports from the bars and existing fasteners. The details use exact reflected geometry.

The sharper grip spikes, straight receiver edges, sword handle, shield, scope, bow, jet, magazines and stock hardware continue from v21. The SVG uses named native paths, shapes, groups, clipping geometry and mirrored references, with no embedded images or font dependencies. The PNG is rendered directly from the same SVG master.
