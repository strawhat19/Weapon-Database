# Weapon Database — Logo Concepts v2

This round develops the two favorites from v1: Armory Crest as the primary direction, with Modular WD as the customization-focused alternative. The Weapon Database name and WD initials continue throughout the round.

| Direction | Editable files | Changes and likely use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 01 — Armory Crest | `01-armory-crest-mark.svg`, `01-armory-crest-lockup.svg` | A peaked armory shield, silver-white central sword, green rifles, and a scope reticle. A fighter jet and bow-and-arrow motif broaden the collection to air and ranged weapons. Primary identity, header, collection crest, or larger app icon. |
| 02 — Modular WD | `02-modular-wd-mark.svg`, `02-modular-wd-lockup.svg` | Green WD armor panels with pale crossed blades, angular frame details, targeting brackets, and finish swatches. Emphasizes customization, camouflage, skins, and melee equipment. App icon, customization tools, or compact identity. |

The larger emblem versions deliberately include more detail than v1. The jet, bow, targeting marks, and finish swatches are secondary elements; the crest and WD remain the main silhouettes. A simplified small-size production variant can be developed when a final direction is chosen.

| Color | Hex | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Vault Black | `#090E0B` | Emblem bodies, gaps, and presentation background |
| Field Olive | `#617340` | Shield frame, weapon silhouettes, and secondary panels |
| Tactical Green | `#8AAD45` | Monogram and primary accents |
| Structural Green | `#3E5949` | Targeting lines, armor details, and supporting structure |
| Archive Light / Silver White | `#E6EDDF` | Sword and dagger blades, contrasting details, and wordmark |

SVG artwork has transparent surrounding canvases, accessible titles and descriptions, and editable geometry. Marks use a `256 × 256` viewBox; lockups use `920 × 280`. Lockup text is intended for a dark background and remains live editable text using Arial Black, Impact, and sans-serif fallbacks.

`03-armory-modular-refinement.png` is the visual comparison sheet edited with the built-in image generation tool from `../v1/04-military-logo-exploration.png`. Its exact instructions are saved in `generation-prompt.txt`. It is a reference-based visual refinement; the SVG files are independent editable interpretations of the same requested motifs, rather than exact traces of the raster artwork.

All files are new in `assets/concepts/logos/v2/`. Earlier rounds are preserved. Verification, testing, builds, and application adoption are left to the project owner, as requested.
